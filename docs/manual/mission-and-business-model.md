# Mission and business model

**ERIC v0.1.1 is BETA software.** Use it only with hardware for testing purposes in safe conditions. It is provided AS IS with no warranty.

## What we are trying to do

The goal is to remove the need to pay a license fee for industrial software, to shorten the time it takes to build a plant application, and to let a company take the source, change it, and ship their own version.

ERIC is the gateway, tags, history, alarms and runtime. Control Canvas is the screen designer. Both are free to run.

## The license, in plain words

The source is AGPL-3.0-or-later. Copyright is Nick Hepburn. He is the only maintainer.

- You may use it, change it, and give it to other people, including inside a company.
- If you distribute a changed copy, or you serve a changed copy over a network (the gateway does that), you must publish your changes under the same license.
- Contributions use the contributor agreement (CLA.md). That agreement lets the maintainer relicense a contribution and includes a patent grant.
- An extra permission lets ERIC be linked with libraries under the Eclipse Public License (EPL-2.0 and EPL-1.0). Those libraries stay under the EPL. The ERIC code stays under the AGPL. See LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md.

The names ERIC and Control Canvas, and the logos, are trademarks. A fork may use the source. A fork may not use those names or logos. See TRADEMARKS.md.

## How it is paid for

The aim is close to not-for-profit. Money that comes in pays for development and testing. What is left goes back into the product. The books stay open.

Revenue is planned from three places, none of which are built in this beta:

- Crowd-funded features on the [future features](wishlist.md) list.
- Paid support credits.
- A paid support assistant that reads a log or a backup you upload.

The full plan is [Funding and support](funding.md). Paying does not buy a private copy of the ERIC or Control Canvas names.
